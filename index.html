About Me

I've worked in tech for over 25 years. I moved to San Francisco in 2008 to be a senior developer at Digg. Since then I've co-founded Onesheet, a simple web presence for artists that Bandzoogle acquired in 2013, advised FitStar through its acquisition by Fitbit, and managed two engineering teams at SunPower that built its Instant Design and Advanced Design tools for solar roofs.

These days I build Merch.link, which I started in 2014 and rebuilt from the ground up this fall. Artists, bands and small businesses get a free merch store: they upload a design and set a price, and we print and ship every order, with no inventory and nothing up front. Since the relaunch I've added MerchTable, so fans at a show scan a QR code on the merch table, pay on their phone and get it shipped home, plus night-by-night stats for every store, a new merch editor and one-step checkout.

I'm also a developer at SeaPeople, a social network for boaters and sailors, and the founder of TheBetX, an options-style exchange for sports betting contracts with three patents behind it. Lately I've been building Kivent, one shared record for every live show that venues, promoters, agents, managers and bands all work from, and Skipper's Locker, marine parts search with plain-English pages on what to fit. And I rebuilt 606mag.com, sixosix, the Chicago web magazine from 2004, from the Wayback Machine and old server backups.

I've played music all my life, including playing and touring with Great Apes, and these days you'll usually find me on my sailboat.
